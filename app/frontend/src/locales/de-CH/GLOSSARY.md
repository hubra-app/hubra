# Swiss German Terminology Glossary

## Conventions (general Swiss German dialect — not region-specific)

### Spelling

- `wärde` instead of `werde`
- `gmeinsam` instead of `gemeinsam`
- `isch` instead of `ist`
- `bisch` instead of `bist`
- `gärn` instead of `gerne`
- `eus` instead of `uns`
- Double vowels for emphasis: `Aabe`, `Ruum`, `Boot`
- English terms stay English: Login, Dashboard, Mail, Tracking
- Nouns near-standard (capitalized)

### Register

- Informal "Du" throughout
- Casual, direct tone
- Use club-internal terms consistently
