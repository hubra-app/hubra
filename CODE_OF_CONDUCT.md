# Code of conduct

Hubra is software for clubs, and clubs work because people treat each other
well. We ask the same of everyone who takes part here: in issues, pull
requests, discussions, reviews, on the support site and in any other space
run by the Hubra project.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Hubra to be free of harassment for everyone,
whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and talk in ways that keep this an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy community.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new here or who write
  in a language that is not their first. Hubra is used in German and
  English; either is fine.
- Respect other opinions and experience, and disagree about the work, not
  the person.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Say what you would change and why.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to the people they affected, and learn from
  them.
- Think about the club members whose data runs through Hubra. Do not post
  real member data, screenshots with personal details, or credentials in an
  issue or pull request.

## What we do not accept

- Sexualised language or images, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a postal or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every Hubra space: the repositories under
[hubra-app](https://github.com/hubra-app), the support site at
[support.hubra.club](https://support.hubra.club), and any chat or forum the
project runs. It also applies when someone represents Hubra in public, for example
from an official account or at an event.

It does not decide disputes inside a club that uses Hubra. Each club has
its own rules for that.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, or you are not sure whether they did, please
tell us: write to **[hi@hubra.club](mailto:hi@hubra.club)** with "Code of conduct" in the subject.

Include what happened, where (links help), when, and who was involved. You
can report something that happened to someone else.

Reports are read only by the project maintainers. We keep the reporter's
identity private and share details only with the people who need them to
handle the report. A maintainer who is part of the report steps aside from
handling it.

We confirm that we got your report within **3 working days**.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide what to do about a report and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this
code. They use these steps as a guide:

1. **Correction.** For unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour: a private,
   written note that explains what was wrong. A public apology may be
   requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single serious incident or repeated behaviour: a
   warning with consequences if it continues. No contact with the people
   involved for a set time, including in other Hubra spaces.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious breach, including sustained
   inappropriate behaviour: a ban from any interaction with the project for a
   set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of breaches, harassment of a person, or
   aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban
   from the project.
